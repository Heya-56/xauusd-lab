## 3. Data sources and limitations

- No verified XAUUSD spot/CFD history is available to the repository or CI.
- Yahoo `XAUUSD=X` returns 404; `GC=F` is COMEX futures and is never reported as spot.
- IBKR needs an authenticated local gateway.
- Consequence: **no XAUUSD spot/CFD backtest result exists or is claimed.** Real-mode research
  reports `DATA_MISSING` until a dataset is placed in `data/verified/` (format in its README).
